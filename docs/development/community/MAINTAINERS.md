## Responsibilities

- Review and merge PRs
- Enforce architecture contracts
- Cut releases
- Manage security disclosures
- Keep documentation accurate

## Becoming a Maintainer

1. Consistent quality contributions over 3+ months
2. Understanding of the 9 kernel subsystems
3. Nomination by existing maintainer
4. Agreement to governance and code of conduct

## Maintainer Emeritus

Past maintainers who are no longer active but contributed significantly are listed as Emeritus with gratitude.
